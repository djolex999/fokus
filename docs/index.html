Catch the thought before you follow it.

You are working. Reply to that email. Check that invoice. Following it costs you the session. fokus gives you somewhere to put it, and puts your cursor back where it was.

What it does

  • Sessions

    A task and 25 or 50 minutes. The countdown comes from the wall clock, so it survives sleep. Stopping early is recorded as data, never as failure — there is no red X anywhere in this app.

  • Capture

    One line, one keystroke, focus returns. You see return 3 for a second, then nothing.

  • Come back

    Away five minutes or more and the widget shows your task and the last three things you wrote down, so you can pick up the thread without opening anything.

  • Evidence

    Fourteen days of your own sessions: when you start, how long you last, how often you break off. Printable as one page.
